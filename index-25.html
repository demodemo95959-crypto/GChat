👻 Ghost Chat — Privacy Policy

Ghost Chat is a personal, non-commercial project built by Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio) (Velqon Studio). It is not a business product: there are no ads, no payments, no selling of data — ever. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

🚫 No ads 💸 Not commercial 🛡️ No data selling 👤 Made by Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio)

Last updated: September 2026 · Version 1.0

✦Short summary (Hinglish)

  • Ye app sirf shauk aur seekhne ke liye Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio) ne banaya hai — koi business ya kamai ka project nahi hai.
  • Aapki chat aapke aur samne wale ke beech hoti hai. Koi random user ya bahar ka insaan aapki chat nahi dekh sakta.
  • Sirf Akshat (admin) zarurat padne par chat dekh sakta hai — security ke liye, ya kisi complaint / allegation / kanooni case ki halat me. Bina wajah ke chats padhna uska maqsad nahi hai.
  • Aapka data kisi ko becha nahi jata aur kisi advertising company ko nahi diya jata.
  • Apna real naam / asli identity share mat kariye — ye ek "ghost" app hai 👻

1About this project

Ghost Chat is a small messaging app created and maintained by Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio) as an independent, hobby / learning project. It is not a commercial service. Nobody pays to use it and nobody is paid through it. The app is provided "as is", free of charge, with best-effort care but no guarantees of uptime or permanence.

2Who is responsible

The person responsible for this app and for how your data is handled is Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio) (the "Admin", "we", "us"). If you have a question, request or complaint, message the admin account inside the app.

3What information we store

  • Account details: the username you choose, your password (handled by our authentication provider — we never see it in plain text), and the gender you select.
  • Profile details: profile photo, bio, badge / rank labels, and your account visibility setting (Public / Private).
  • Messages: text messages, voice notes, photos and files you send, with the time they were sent.
  • Social data: friend requests, friends list and the people you have blocked.
  • App settings: chat background, in-app PIN lock, pinned / hidden chats, read status and which chats you cleared.
  • Notification data: if you turn on notifications, a push-subscription token from your browser.
  • Online status: whether you are online right now (shown to other users while you are active).
✅ We do not ask for your email, phone number, address, contacts, real name or location. Ghost Chat uses an internal username system.

4Who can see your chats

Your conversations are visible to you and the person you are talking to. Other users, friends of friends, and the public cannot read your chats.

The Admin (Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio)) has technical access to the database and can view conversations. This access exists only for:

  • keeping the platform and its users safe (harassment, threats, abuse, scams, illegal content);
  • investigating a complaint, report or allegation against a user or against the admin;
  • responding to a valid legal request or court / police process;
  • fixing technical problems and testing the app.
⚠️ Ghost Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. Messages are protected in transit (HTTPS) and stored in a secured database, but the Admin can technically read them. Please do not share passwords, bank details, ID numbers or anything you would not want an admin to be able to see.

5Admin moderation powers (full transparency)

To run and protect the community, the Admin can use an admin panel to:

  • view any conversation, including messages that were deleted or cleared;
  • edit or remove messages that break the rules;
  • change or remove a profile photo, bio, username, badge or rank;
  • ban / unban accounts, block or unblock users, and manage friend links;
  • send messages from managed or support accounts, and create accounts.

These tools are for moderation, support and testing. They are not used for advertising, profiling or selling anything.

6Public and Private accounts

  • Public account 🌍 — anyone can open your profile, see your bio and friends, and add you as a friend instantly.
  • Private account 🔒 — only approved friends can see your bio and friends list. Others must send a request and you decide whether to accept.

You can switch at any time from Settings → Account Privacy. Your username and profile photo remain visible in search so people can find and request you. Only friends can message you.

7Friends, blocking and messaging

You can only chat with users who are your friends. If you block someone, they cannot message or call you. Blocking is stored so it keeps working across your devices. The Admin may also block, unblock or connect accounts for moderation purposes.

8Deleting messages and "Clear All Chat"

  • Delete for me hides a message from your screen only.
  • Delete for everyone replaces your message with "This message was deleted" for both people.
  • Clear All Chat (Settings) wipes the whole conversation with the person you select — from your side only. The other person still has their copy.
📌 Deleted or cleared content disappears from the app for you, but a copy may remain on our servers and can be viewed by the Admin for safety and legal reasons (see section 4).

9Photos, files and voice notes

Attachments (up to 15 MB) are uploaded to our storage and shared through a link that only the app is meant to show inside the chat. Please remember that anyone who receives a file can save or forward it, and we cannot control what they do with it after delivery.

10Voice and video calls

Calls use WebRTC. Audio and video normally travel directly between the two devices; our server only passes the small set-up messages needed to connect the call. Calls are not recorded or stored. Your browser will ask for microphone / camera permission and you can deny it at any time.

11Ghost AI assistant

The Ghost AI helper in your chat list answers questions only about how to use Ghost Chat. It runs inside the app, does not read your private chats, and what you type to it is not sent to any outside AI company. Your conversation with Ghost AI is kept on your own device only, and it disappears when you clear the app's data.

12Notifications

If you allow notifications, your browser gives us a push token so we can alert you about new messages even when the app is closed. You can switch this off any time in your browser / phone settings.

13Security

We use a reputable backend platform (Supabase) with HTTPS connections, access rules on the database and password hashing. Your optional in-app PIN only locks the screen on your device; it is a convenience lock, not encryption. No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share it.

14Third-party services

Ghost Chat relies on a few outside services to work: Supabase (database, login, file storage and realtime), GitHub Pages (or similar static hosting) (to serve the app), Google Fonts (fonts) and public STUN servers (to help calls connect). These providers may process technical data such as IP address as part of running their service. We do not sell your data to any of them or to anyone else.

15How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists so the app can work. Because this is a small personal project there is no automatic advertising or analytics profile. If you want your account and data removed, ask the Admin and it will be deleted as soon as reasonably possible, except where we must keep information for safety or legal reasons.

16Your choices and rights

  • Edit your username, photo, bio, gender and password anytime.
  • Switch your account between Public and Private.
  • Block users, remove friends, delete messages, clear chats.
  • Turn notifications and the PIN lock on or off.
  • Ask the Admin to show, correct or delete your data.

17Rules and safety

Do not use your real name or real identity. Harassment, hate, threats, sexual content involving minors, scams, illegal activity or impersonation are not allowed. Accounts that break the rules can be warned, banned or removed by the Admin or by automated moderation. If we believe someone is in danger or a crime is involved, information may be shared with the relevant authorities.

18Age

Ghost Chat is intended for teens and adults who can use a chat app responsibly. It is not designed for young children. If you are under the age of majority in your country, please use it with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

19Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. Continuing to use Ghost Chat after an update means you accept the new version.

20Contact

Questions, complaints or data requests? Message the admin account inside Ghost Chat, or ask Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio) directly.

💜 Thank you for using Ghost Chat. — Akshat Dubey (Velqon Studio), Velqon Studio
